5-Step Tantrum Calm-Down Protocol

Step 1: Lower Your Voice & Body

Get down to their eye level. Speak 30% softer than usual.

Step 2: Name the Emotion

Say: "You really wanted that toy, and it's hard when we have to stop playing."

Step 3: Hold the Boundary Gently

Say: "It is okay to be upset, but it is not okay to hit."

Step 4: Offer Physical Anchor

Offer an open embrace or sit quietly beside them until the big cry settles.

Step 5: Connect Before Teaching

Once calm, offer water, take three deep belly breaths together, then talk.
