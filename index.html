5-Step Tantrum Protocol

Step 1: Lower Your Voice & BodyGet down to eye level. Speak 30% softer than usual.
Step 2: Name the EmotionSay: "You really wanted that toy, it is hard to stop."
Step 3: Hold Boundary GentlySay: "It is okay to be upset, but not okay to hit."
Step 4: Offer Physical AnchorOffer an open hug or sit quietly beside them until crying settles.
Step 5: Connect Before TeachingOffer water, take 3 deep belly breaths together before talking.
